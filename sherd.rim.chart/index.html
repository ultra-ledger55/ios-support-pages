Sherd Arc

Sherd Arc measures a pottery rim and totals the sherds in a context.

A rim

Lay the rim face-down next to a scale bar and photograph it from straight above. Drag the two ends of the scale bar, then drag points along the rim edge. The circle fit gives a diameter and the surviving arc. EVE is that arc divided by 360 degrees.

A context

Rims of the same ware and form, with diameters within 1 cm, are grouped as possible vessels. The context summary lists EVE by ware and form, a diameter chart, and an MNV estimate. Contexts line up in section order so you can compare them.

On this phone

Photographs and records stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Check the diameter against the rim in front of you before you record it.
