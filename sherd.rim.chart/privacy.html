Privacy Policy

Sherd Arc measures pottery rim sherds and totals them by context. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

Site codes, context numbers, ware and form names, rim diameters, surviving arcs, and rim photographs are stored on this iPhone. Sherd Arc does not ask you to create an account.

What Sherd Arc does not collect

Sherd Arc does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
