Streif Window

Streif Window reads starch on cut apples and shows which block to pick first.

A sample

Pick ten fruit from one block. Cut them, stain the halves, and match the clear edge on the starch chart. Firmness is the sun face and the shade face. Sugar is Brix. The Streif index is firmness divided by Brix times starch.

The window

Each variety has an enter value and a leave value. The mean of the ten halves says the block is not yet in the window, inside it, or past it. Blocks are ordered by which one leaves the window first.

On this phone

Blocks and samples stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Check the reading against the halves in front of you before you pick.
