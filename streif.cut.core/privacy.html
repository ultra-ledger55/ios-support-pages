Privacy Policy

Streif Window times the harvest window for each block in an orchard. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

Block names, variety windows, sample dates, starch grades, firmness, Brix, and tray photos are stored on this iPhone. Streif Window does not ask you to create an account.

What Streif Window does not collect

Streif Window does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
