Privacy Policy

Plate Map lays out a 96-well qPCR plate. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

Plate names, sample names, target names, replicate counts, well assignments, mix volumes, and which wells you have already loaded are stored on this iPhone. Plate Map does not ask you to create an account.

What Plate Map does not collect

Plate Map does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
