Plate Map

Plate Map lays out a 96-well qPCR plate, mixes each target, and keeps your place while you pipette.

The plate

Samples and targets are written in. Replicates stay together down a column, and each target can keep an NTC well. Wells that do not fit are listed before you start loading.

The mix and the load

Each target gets a master mix from the wells on the plate, plus an overage. Template is left out of that mix and added well by well. Tap a well after you dispense it. Leave and come back, and the next well is still marked.

On this phone

Plates stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Check the plate map against the plate in front of you before you pipette.
