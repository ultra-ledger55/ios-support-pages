Core RQD

Core RQD packs a drill run into a core box and reads the rock quality designation.

A run

Enter the depth from and to, and the length of core recovered. The run fills the next rows of a wooden box, five rows to a box. Tap a row and mark natural or mechanical breaks. Drag a lost-core gap or a rubble zone along the recovered length.

The numbers

RQD is the share of drilled length that comes back in pieces of 10 cm or longer. TCR is recovered length over drilled length. SCR leaves out rubble. The fracture frequency counts natural breaks along the recovered core. The grade runs from Very poor to Excellent.

A hole

Lithology intervals split the hole. The log draws RQD against depth. Several holes line up by collar elevation so you can compare one contact across the site.

On this phone

Records and box photographs stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Check the recovered length against the core in the box before you save the run.
