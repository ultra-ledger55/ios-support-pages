Privacy Policy

Core RQD logs drill core and reads RQD for each run. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

Hole names, site names, collar elevations, run depths, recovered lengths, breaks, rubble zones, lithology intervals, and box photographs are stored on this iPhone. Core RQD does not ask you to create an account.

What Core RQD does not collect

Core RQD does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Camera and photos

A box photograph is taken or chosen only when you tap the camera on that box. The picture stays with the hole on this iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
